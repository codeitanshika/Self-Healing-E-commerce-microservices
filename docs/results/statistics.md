## How to read this honestly

- Every trial includes a fixed 2s validation wait, so MTTR cannot fall below ~2s; differences between models are small relative to that floor.
- Statistical significance is not practical significance: even a significant MTTR gap of a few tenths of a second is irrelevant next to a 900s manual baseline.
- The faults are simulated and drawn from four classes; accuracy near 100% reflects an easy taxonomy, not a general guarantee.
